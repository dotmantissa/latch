# Latch

Challenge: Blast Radius Zero, Vultr Agent Rush Hackathon.

Short description: Inspect public supplier offers in a contained browser, verify captured evidence with Vultr inference, and keep the final decision with the requester.

A missing accessory, conflicting price or unquoted shipping charge can make an equipment purchase fail before it arrives. Latch puts item descriptions, quantities, budget and deadline beside attributable supplier evidence. The agent plans a bounded inspection of already admitted public pages, executes Playwright in a disposable gVisor container, extracts provisional terms and checks screenshots with vision inference. People review individual fields and compare complete baskets. Approval binds the saved version and expires; a fresh recheck must pass before an immutable internal decision receipt can be committed.

Vultr runs both the public HTTPS app and its separate sandbox worker. All planning, text extraction and image correspondence calls use Vultr Serverless Inference. Neon stores durable records. The worker receives no inference, database or deployment credentials. Browser egress permits only reviewed public pages and static asset paths. Network denial, host watchdog termination and cleanup are tested on the actual Vultr worker.

The public demo uses the real Ubiquiti U6+ page, with explicitly labelled demonstration requirements. Current evidence has unresolved terms and unsupported product photographs; those conditions block approval. This is evidence review, not an order or a claim of buyer acceptance. Demo visitors receive isolated sessions and at most two live inspections. Named operator accounts support the full approval and receipt workflow.
